Variables, conditions and scripts live at the top of a layout document, keyed by canvas id
(`"variables": {"<canvas id>" | "page_variables": [...]}`, `"conditions": {"<canvas id>": [...]}`), or as a list
(`"scripts"`). See the guide's {{link:index.md#guide-variables|variables}} and
{{link:index.md#guide-conditions|conditions}} sections for examples.
